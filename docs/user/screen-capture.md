# Screen capture

A screen capture records the monitor under your mouse with your voice, your clicks, and red ink, then
puts the result into your draft: what you said next to the moments on screen, and a contact sheet of
those moments. It is available in the desktop app. Clicks and ink need Windows.

## Taking a capture

Press **Alt+Shift+S** in any app, select the screen button in the composer, or choose **Capture the
screen** in the command palette. Talk and click as you normally would, and hold **Shift** while
moving the mouse to draw. Press the shortcut again to finish, or Esc in T3 Code to discard.

## Changing the shortcut

Open **Settings** > **SnapShots** > **Screen capture**, select the shortcut, press the new keys, then
**Save**. If another program is holding the shortcut, that row says so. T3 Code takes it as soon as
it is free, and the composer button and command palette work until then.

## Finding a past capture

Press **Alt+Shift+H**, choose **Past screen captures** in the command palette, or select **Show
captures** in Settings. From there you can put a capture back into your draft, copy its text, or open
its folder. Each row shows what happens to it next.

## What gets deleted

By default a capture's video and audio go to the Recycle Bin after 7 days. Its text and images stay
until the whole capture goes after 30 days. Change this under **Keep screen captures**, or pin a
capture in **Past screen captures** to keep it.

## After a crash

If the app closes or reloads during a capture, the next launch builds what was recorded and tells you
when it is done. The last few seconds may be missing. A capture that cannot be built stays in the list
with a rebuild button.
